Privacy policy

X-agent local pipeline · Last updated: October 10, 2026

Data processed

When Google is connected, the application receives OAuth tokens for access to the YouTube API. It requests caption metadata for public videos, including track identifiers, languages, types, and availability. Public video information and available caption text are also processed when preparing catalog content.

Purpose

This information is used to select caption languages, assess reviews and demonstrations, and prepare source-supported content about music products. Google authorization is not used to change YouTube account data.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and the client secret are stored locally on the operator's computer in a file with restricted access permissions. Tokens are not included in AI model requests or published in the catalog. The application communicates with Google to obtain and refresh tokens.

Public text, including captions, may be sent to the AI provider configured by the operator for fact extraction and moderation. Prepared content may be published in the catalog after a separate operator action. OAuth tokens are not shared with these providers.

Retention and control

Local materials are retained until the operator deletes them. The operator can delete collected data and disconnect YouTube in X-agent's settings. Disconnecting removes local tokens and sends a request to Google to revoke access. Access can also be revoked directly in Google account settings.
