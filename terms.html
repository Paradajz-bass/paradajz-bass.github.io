Application purpose

X-agent is intended for one operator to prepare and review music product catalog content locally. This website provides information about the application.

Connecting Google

The operator authorizes access to the YouTube API to retrieve caption track lists. Access can be disconnected in X-agent or revoked in Google account settings.

Reviewing results

Content prepared through automated processing and AI may contain errors. The operator reviews results before publication and makes a separate decision to publish them.

Using sources

The operator must follow applicable service rules and respect the rights of content owners. Google authorization does not grant permission to edit other users' videos or redistribute their content.
